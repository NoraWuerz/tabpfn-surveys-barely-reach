# 08_recs_electricity -- RECS 2020

**Source.** U.S. Energy Information Administration (2022). 2020 Residential
Energy Consumption Survey (RECS): Public Use Microdata File, Version 7.
Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Energy.

**Access.** Open download,
https://www.eia.gov/consumption/residential/data/2020/index.php?view=microdata
(file `recs2020_public_v7.csv`).

**Terms.** Work of the U.S. federal government, public domain (17 U.S.C. 105).
Redistribution permitted. Suggested acknowledgement (EIA asks for the
publication date): "Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (2022),
RECS 2020 public-use microdata v7; modified extract."

## Deposit tier: population

`data/` holds the prepared source extract, the fixed population and the 50
stored sample draws; `results/` holds the unit-level predictions of the 11
estimators. The deposited files are a modified extract of the source, not the
provider's original files: extract definition, filters and counts are given in
SI Appendix, Section S3 of the paper.
